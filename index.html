What to expect

First visit or fiftieth, the appointment runs the same way, so you always know what you're walking out with.

  1. Consult

    We talk about your routine, what's bugging you about your hair, and photos if you have them.

  2. Cut

    April cuts to your texture, cowlicks, and growth pattern, not a one-size template.

  3. Finish

    A style-through that shows you how to get the same look at home, with the products that do it.

  4. Rebook

    Leave with your next visit set at the right interval for the cut, usually 4 to 8 weeks.
